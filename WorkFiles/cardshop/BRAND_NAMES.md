# Card Shop Kit: fictional brand names (D3)

**Date:** 2026-09-28. **Status:** names **picked by the user 2026-09-28** (the ★ recommendations, section 4). **USPTO screen still pending**
(section 3, the user); no brand art is drawn until it passes (`CARDSHOP_KIT_SPEC.md` 6.1 rule 1, gate G0).
**Scope:** the 8 names the spec needs (section 4.1 of the spec): 2 fantasy card lines, 1 sports-style line with a
fictional sport, 2 graders, 1 accessory brand, 1 distributor, 1 demo shop.

**What was checked.** Each name below had a web search (2026-09-28, cloud session) for existing games, card games,
card-hobby businesses and brands. 49 names were tried in 4 rounds; the 26 that hit something were dropped (section 2). A
web search is **not** a trademark clearance: it misses unregistered local shops and does not read the USPTO register.
Not legal advice.

**Rules the names follow.** Coined or compound words that describe nothing real; no franchise roots or sound-alikes
(no -mon, Magic, Gathering, Duel, Trainer, Lorcana, Topps, Panini, Chrome, Prizm); no three-letter grader acronyms (that
space is crowded: PSA, BGS, CGC, SGC, TAG, AGS, HGA, ACE...); no two picks sharing a root (e.g. not Ashvow + Ashgrove).

## 3. USPTO screen (manual, by the user)

For each picked name, plus "toploader" and "top-loader" (spec 6.3 gate 4):

1. Open the USPTO trademark search (uspto.gov → Trademarks → Search trademarks).
2. Search the word mark exactly, then with spaces and hyphens (e.g. `Pyrecall`, `Pyre Call`, `Pyre-call`).
3. Look for **live** marks in the classes that touch this kit: **28** (games, trading cards for games, card sleeves as game
   accessories), **16** (printed matter, collectible cards, paper goods), **9** (downloadable game software), **35** (retail
   and wholesale distribution), **41** (entertainment, tournaments), **42/36** (authentication and appraisal, for the
   grader names).
4. Pass = no live mark that is identical or confusingly similar in those classes. If one fails, fall back to the next
   option in its row, or ask for new names.
5. Fill in section 4.
