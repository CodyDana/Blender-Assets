# SMG01 (Compact SMG SM-01): reference measurement and tracing notes

The 2D outline authority for the SMG01 build (`SK_SMG01`, `SM_SMG01`, `SM_SMG01_Mag`). Source:
`References/Guns/SMG/smg_sheetA.webp` (1254x1254, AI-generated by the user; Sheet A only). Everything below is
regenerated in about 10 s by:

    py -3 -B WorkFiles/guns/smg/tools/trace_reference.py

The tool is the AR-01 tracer adapted (`tools/trace_reference.py` + `tools/refimg.py`, numpy/scipy/Pillow only, paths
from its own location). `tools/gridzoom.py` makes the gridded zooms the hand-read seams came from.

## Frame

- **mm:** +X toward the muzzle, +Z up, +Y = the gun's left (right-handed; the line standard, study 5.1).
  **X = 0 at the rearmost point of the butt pad, stock extended. Z = 0 on the bore axis. Y = 0 on the mid-plane.**
- **Bore axis:** its *direction* is the top rail's tooth-top line (14 teeth, fit rms 0.12 px, the longest straight edge
  on the gun); its *height* is the muzzle device / exposed barrel centre line. The device body centre sits at
  Z -0.4 mm and the exposed neck at +1.9 mm on that line (RIGHT: -0.6 / +2.9). A straight fit through the device
  alone tilts 3.6 deg (LEFT) / 5.6 deg (RIGHT) against the rail over only 45 px: the device is drawn slightly drooped,
  so the rail decides the direction.
- **px:** pixel-centre coordinates in each panel crop (boxes in `smg_measured.json`). RIGHT is mirrored before tracing so
  that both side views have the muzzle image-left; RIGHT CONFIG coordinates are raw.
- Each view has its own frame (own axis, own X = 0, own scale to 690 mm). Views are compared in mm without fitting.
- For the build's own origin (`Grip`, study 5.1), `reference_points.Grip_origin_provisional_XZ` = (338.0, -91.3): on
  the grip's centre line, 40 mm down from the web line (Z -53.8, where the grip front meets the trigger guard). It is
  provisional until H1.

## Method (as the AR-01, adapted)

1. **Panels** by pixel analysis: rows/columns more than half non-white give the four panels: LEFT (7,7)-(622,570),
   RIGHT (630,7)-(1247,570), TOP (7,611)-(622,1207), 3/4 (630,611)-(1247,1207).
2. **Backdrop:** a robust degree-4 polynomial fit to the grey studio backdrop; residual sd 1.6 / 1.7 / 2.5 grey levels
   (LEFT / RIGHT / TOP). The cm ruler (y >= 440 in the side crops) is excluded.
3. **Soft field** (40 levels darker, chroma 24, or 24 brighter than the backdrop), marching squares at 0.5
   (sub-pixel, oriented rings), Douglas-Peucker 0.25 px. The gun is matte black on light grey, so no force-foreground
   or strict patches were needed (the rifle needed both).
4. **Holes:** every enclosed region is filled except seeded real see-through holes, each checked on 7-10x zooms:
   the trigger guard opening, the two stock windows, and (LEFT only) a 1 px see-through gap between the receiver's
   hinge block and the stock knuckle. RIGHT draws no hinge gap.
5. **Magazine cut** along the dark lower edge of the magwell lip, (172,258)-(214,266) LEFT /
   (452,259.5)-(396,265.5) RIGHT raw.
6. **Parts (LEFT)**: the silhouette inside hand-placed seam regions, first match wins (seams read off 6-10x zooms,
   +-1 px); the stock frame is split automatically from its two windows (top strut below the line through the window
   tops, lower strut above the line through the window bottoms, neck / web / rear upright by the window X ranges).
   Outer edges come from the soft field (sub-pixel); seams from a smoothed label map; 0 px unassigned.
7. **Face features** (inside the silhouette; outline polygons, +-1 px): charging handle knob, rod and rear block, the
   front upper slot, selector pivot ring and lever (both sides), three pins, the ejection port and the boss in front of
   it (RIGHT). Dark slots and vents are also listed automatically (`dark_features_auto`, unlabelled).
8. **Measurements** on the sub-pixel rings (line/ring intersections), never on pixel masks.

QA: I looked at the overlay and every 4x tile (`scratch/qa_zoom_*`). The trace hugs the edges everywhere, including
the rail teeth, the flip sights, the stock windows, the butt pad's ribbed rear face, the floor plate and the trigger.

## Scale

**Chosen: 690 mm overall, stock extended** (butt pad rear to muzzle device tip): LEFT 0.85114 px/mm (1.175 mm/px),
RIGHT 0.85196, TOP 0.84613. This follows the prompt (~690 mm), the real pattern (690 mm) and the line's rule (study
3.5: scale to the overall length).

**The sheet's ruler is wrong:** long ticks every 67.6 px per 10 cm (8 ticks, fit rms 0.3 px), so the gun would be
86.8 cm, +25.9 % (the study's +26 % for SMG01).

**Independent checks** (the sheet's own proportions at 690 vs the real pattern):

| Anchor | Sheet at 690 | Real | Ratio | Overall that would match |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trigger reach (backstrap to blade front face) | 90.5 | ~70 (65-75) | 1.29 | 534 |
| Height with magazine (sight top to floor plate) | 338.3 | 280 (maker's table; magazine not stated) | 1.21 | 571 |
| Top rail pitch, side silhouette (18 teeth, rms 0.44) | 11.05 | 10.01 | 1.10 | 625 |
| Top rail pitch, TOP cross-slot stripes | 11.79 | 10.01 | 1.18 | 586 |
| Receiver width (TOP, plain section) | 68.8 | 61 (maker's table, 2.40 in) | 1.13 | 612 |
| Folded length (muzzle tip to the hinge gap) | 417.6 | 450 | 0.93 | 744 |

No single scale fits them all: the sheet draws a shorter, taller and chunkier gun than the real pattern (bigger grip
and trigger group, taller magazine, thicker body, longer stock). That is the sheet's design and the silhouette gates
keep it. 690 stays the default because it is the headline number the user asked for. The alternative is about 625
(the side rail pitch becomes exactly 10.01 and the width matches 61), but then the overall is -9 % and the folded length
-16 %. Not chosen; open question Q1 below.

The `unc_mm` values are 1-sigma measurement uncertainty only (silhouette +-0.4 px, hand-placed seam +-1 px,
estimate +-2 px); the scale choice is on top.

## Disagreements, real-pattern notes and what the sheet does not show

- **The two side panels are near mirror copies** (IoU 0.947 raw, 0.950 fitted). That makes the side silhouette solid,
  but it is weak proof of the right side's own details. Only the port, the right selector and the boss differ.
- **TOP is the weak view:** the round muzzle device is 26 % wider than tall, the knob sits 7-11 mm further back, the
  hinge about 15 mm further back, and the stock shows both struts side by side. Take only widths from TOP, at +-15 %.
- **The rail is drawn at the wrong pitch** for a 690 mm gun (11.05 side, 11.79 top). The line builds every rail with
  `rail_1913` at 10.01, so the slot count differs from the drawing (19 drawn teeth vs 21 slots on the same span).
- **The magazine leans forward 7.4 deg** in both side views. Keep the angle (silhouette authority); the well, guides
  and catch follow it.
- **Not shown anywhere:** the receiver underside and the magwell opening, the magazine's top (feed lips, follower), the
  inside of the port (bolt face), the folded stock, the hinge's lateral position and latch, the left side of the
  stock hinge from behind, the bore face, the flip sights folded down, and the rail's front and rear end stops. Build
  these from the real pattern and keep them plain.
- **Real-pattern sources used here:** only the published figures (690 / 450 mm, barrel 200 mm, width 2.40 in, height
  11.02 in, 25-round straight box magazine): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heckler_%26_Koch_UMP and
  https://hk-usa.com/hk-models/ump45/. I did not download any photos (that needs the user's go); the real-photo set
  for the small parts belongs to the form stage and goes in `scratch/realref/`.

## Open questions (defaults in brackets)

- **Q1 Scale.** Keep 690 mm and the sheet's chunky middle (trigger reach 90 mm, height 338 mm), or about 625 mm
  where the rail pitch and the width become real? [690; check the hand fit at H1, and take 625 only if H1 fails]
- **Q2 Hidden barrel length.** The sheet's port and magazine put the breech at about X 503, which gives a barrel of
  about 150 mm, not the real 200 mm. [follow the sheet: about 150 mm, crown inside the device collar; nothing visible
  changes]
- **Q3 Rail slots.** Build the standard 10.01 pitch (21 slots between the sights, 26 full length) and accept a
  different count from the drawing? [yes, the line standard]
- **Q4 Magazine thickness.** The 3/4 view gives about 31 +-6. [30 mm, the real .45 double-stack range]
- **Q5 The boss in front of the ejection port (RIGHT, X 511-528).** It is a vertical part with a slot or lever. Keep it
  as a plain moulded boss (no function), or drop it if the real pattern has nothing there? [keep it, plain]
- **Q6 Charging handle path.** The knob sits at X 548 with a rod and block behind it (X 503-542), under the front
  slot. [real pattern: a non-reciprocating left handle that slides straight back in a slot along the upper's left
  side; the travel reaches the bolt's full stroke]
