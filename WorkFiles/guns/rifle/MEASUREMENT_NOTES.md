# AR-01 rifle: reference measurement and tracing notes

The 2D outline authority for the AR-01 build (SM_AR01 + SM_AR01_Mag). Source: `References/Guns/Rifle/rifle_sheetAB.webp`
(1536x1024, AI-generated by the user). Everything below can be regenerated:

    py -3 -B WorkFiles/guns/rifle/tools/trace_reference.py

> script (slivers under 40 px dropped or relabelled to the nearest part; the magazine cut is untouched), and three
> region boundaries were corrected on gridded zooms (the rear sight leaf overhangs the dust cover front; the front sight
> block starts at the dark seam X 806.1; the grip's dark front strap is grip, not trigger guard). The numbers below are
> the regenerated ones. `rifle_spec.json` remains the build authority.

The script needs only numpy, scipy and Pillow, and finds its paths from its own location, so it works in a worktree and
after a merge. It writes:

| Output | What |
|---|---|
| `rifle_measured.json` | All numbers: px/mm per view, panel crop boxes, view-agreement stats, dimensions with uncertainties |
| `trace/LEFT_outline.json` | Silhouette, rifle without magazine, magazine, 21 labelled parts, selector face outline; px and mm |
| `trace/RIGHT_outline.json` | Mirrored RIGHT silhouette, rifle and magazine; px and mm (in RIGHT's own gun frame) |
| `trace/TOP_outline.json` | TOP silhouette, wood parts, width profile every 5 mm |
| `trace/*.png` | Binary masks (silhouette, rifle without magazine, magazine) and the LEFT part label image (index and colour) |
| `RIFLE_TRACE_OVERLAY.png` | QA overlay: traced outlines drawn on the three crops |
| `scratch/crops/*.png` | All 11 panels at full resolution; `scratch/qa_zoom_*.png` are 4x QA tiles (scratch is not committed) |

`.gitignore` ignores `WorkFiles/**/*.png`, so the masks and the overlay stay local unless they are force-added. The
JSON files hold everything the build needs, and the script rebuilds the PNGs in about 5 s.

## Frame

- **mm:** +X toward the muzzle, +Z up, +Y = the gun's left side (right-handed). X = 0 at the rearmost point of the butt
  plate, Z = 0 on the bore axis, Y = 0 on the mid-plane. The bore axis is the barrel centre line, fitted on the exposed
  barrel between the gas block and the front sight block.
- **px:** pixel-centre coordinates inside each panel crop. The crop boxes are in `rifle_measured.json`. RIGHT is
  mirrored before tracing, so all three views have the muzzle on the right.
- Each view gets its own frame, with its own bore axis, butt origin and scale (overall length = 880 mm). Views are
  compared in mm without any fitting.

## Method

1. **Panels.** The panels are found by pixel analysis. The sheet is white, the panels are grey, and captions never
   fill half a row or column. Rows that are more than half non-white give the three bands; inside each band, columns
   that are more than half non-white give the panels; each panel's own top and bottom are refined over its columns.
   This finds 11 panels; the 3 px anti-aliased border of each panel is ignored.
2. **Backdrop.** A robust degree-4 2D polynomial is fitted to the neutral grey studio backdrop. The residual sd is
   1.9 grey levels on the side views and 2.8 on TOP.
3. **Soft foreground field.** A pixel scores 0.5 on the edge when it is 40 levels darker than the backdrop (blued
   steel), when its chroma is 24 or more (wood), or when it is 24 levels brighter (specular highlights). The 0.5
   iso-contour is found with marching squares, which gives sub-pixel, consistently oriented rings. These are simplified
   with Douglas-Peucker at 0.25 px.
4. **Holes.** Every enclosed region is filled except the real see-through holes. Each real hole was checked on
   12x gridded zooms and is kept by a seed point:
   - the rod gaps in front of and behind the gas block
   - the gas tube / barrel gap
   - the front sight hood window
   - the trigger guard opening
   - a 3 mm gap under the rear sight leaf
   
   Long bright highlight bands on the steel reach the backdrop at their ends. They are closed with a 3x3 square plus a
   7 px vertical line before the fill.
5. **Hand patches.** A few patches handle edges that no automatic criterion can see. Each was read off a 12x zoom:
   - **Force-foreground** (top highlights that match the backdrop): the exposed gas tube top, the gas block top and
     the dust cover's rear slope on LEFT; the dust cover top and the gas tube top on RIGHT.
   - **Strict threshold** (soft floor contact shadows): the shadow under the magazine floor plate (both side views)
     and the drop shadow along the image-bottom side of the receiver in TOP.
6. **Parts (LEFT).**
   - Wood parts come from chroma: stock, pistol grip, and the upper and lower handguards (split on their dark seam).
   - Steel parts are the silhouette inside hand-placed seam regions, read off the zooms to about 1 px: butt plate,
     sling swivel, receiver, dust cover, rear sight, gas tube rear cap, handguard front band, gas tube, gas block,
     barrel, cleaning rod, front sight block, muzzle brake, trigger, trigger guard and mag catch.
   - The magazine is cut from the rifle along the receiver's bottom line and in front of the mag catch.
   - Each part's outer edges come from the soft field, so they are sub-pixel. Its seams come from a smoothed label map.
     Only 1 px is left unassigned.
7. **Measurements** are taken on the sub-pixel rings (line and ring intersections), not on pixel masks.

## Scale

- **Chosen: 880 mm overall,** from the rearmost butt plate point to the muzzle brake tip. That is LEFT at
  0.82248 px/mm (1.2158 mm/px), RIGHT at 0.82224 and TOP at 0.81553.
- **The sheet's ruler is not usable.** Its 11 long ticks are labelled 0, 10 ... 90 and then "90 cm" again, 66.7 px
  apart (6.67 px/cm, tick fit rms 0.9 px).
  - Read at face value (10 cm per long tick), the gun would be 108.5 cm.
  - Taking the whole ruler as 90 cm, it would be 97.7 cm.
  - Neither is a real rifle.
- **Why 880 mm:**
  - It is the real fixed-stock stamped-receiver 7.62x39 pattern's length, and the length the prompt asked for.
  - With 880 mm, the sheet's sight radius (rear leaf to front hood centre) is 376.6 mm, against about 378 mm on the
    real pattern (-0.4 %; the spec's notch-to-post radius is 375.4).
  - The receiver front face to the brake rear face is 363.5 mm. That is consistent with a 415 mm barrel, roughly 45-50
    mm of which sits inside the trunnion.
  - So the sheet's internal proportions match the real pattern to within about 2.5 %, and the choice is good to
    about +-1.5 % (`global_scale_uncertainty_pct`).
- **Length of pull** (butt to trigger face) comes out at about 320 mm. That is shorter than the real pattern (about
  340-350 mm). The sheet draws a slightly short stock, or a slightly long front.

**Perspective.** The barrel edges are straight lines to 0.08 px rms. The LEFT bore is level to 0.06 deg. The front
sight hood's front edge is vertical to 1.3 deg. All three views have the same length in px to within 0.8 %. The views
are treated as orthographic. The disagreements in the table are the image generator drawing the gun differently in
each panel, not projection. The TOP panel's barrel is drawn about 0.4 deg off the rest of the gun, so the TOP frame
uses the gun's overall centre line (stock, handguard and barrel centres).

## Disagreements and what the sheet does not show

- **The LEFT view shows a long lever on the left side.** It pivots at the rear and points forward, like a selector.
  The RIGHT and 3/4 views show the same lever on the right, which is where the real pattern has it. The left one is
  the generator mirroring the right side. It is traced as a face feature; whether to build it is a user decision.
- **TOP's lateral features don't match the side views.** Image-up shows a flat plate (X 353-462, 20 mm beyond the
  body), a small step at X 236-295 and a nub at X 471-481. Image-down shows a knob (X 470-482, 12.7 mm). The 3/4 view
  puts the charging handle at the front of a right-side ejection port. Image-up would be the gun's right only if TOP is
  mirrored. Widths are not affected, but don't take lateral features from TOP.
- **The "magazine (left)" close-up curves the wrong way** (front edge on the left). It is really a right-side view,
  and is used for the rib pattern only.
- **Close-ups are not to scale.** The magazine front and butt end ratios give thinner parts than TOP does.
- **RIGHT's middle section sits 12-16 mm forward of LEFT's.** LEFT wins, because TOP sides with LEFT.
- **Not shown anywhere:**
  - the receiver underside and mag well
  - handguard, stock and magazine cross-sections (only TOP widths and the end view)
  - the inside of the brake slot beyond what the muzzle close-up shows
  - the front sight post (hidden in the hood)
  - the left side of the gas block
  - the dust cover's rear button
  - the bore face
  
  Keep these plain and plausible; leaf graduations and markings are left off on purpose (no markings).
- **Ambiguous see-through holes.** The 3 mm gap under the rear sight leaf and the 3-5 px rod and gas tube gaps are
  real in the image. They are small, so the build may close them if they read poorly in game.

## Changes that would make a Fab version the user's own design (not applied)

The sheet is a near-replica of a real design. Small original changes the user could choose for a Fab version:

1. **Muzzle:** replace the slant brake with an original device, e.g. a round two-port brake or a cylinder with 3
   short side slots.
2. **Butt plate:** a rubber pad with 3 horizontal ribs and no screws, instead of the steel stadium plate with 2 screws.
3. **Stock:** a smoother comb, without the stepped comb nose and wrist dip. Optionally add a steel tang strap.
4. **Handguards:** a different vent and slot pattern on the lower guard (e.g. 3 short slots), and a single finger
   groove instead of the two long slots on the upper guard.
5. **Gas block:** a squared front instead of the 45 deg drop; round holes on the gas tube instead of slots.
6. **Receiver side:** a rectangular recess instead of the oval mag-well dimple, and a different rivet layout.
7. **Magazine:** a different rib layout (3 ribs plus a stamped waffle panel) and floor plate outline.
8. **Pistol grip:** diamond checkering and a finger swell instead of the vertical groove panel.
9. **Front sight:** a closed ring hood instead of the open ears; a different rear sight leaf and slider profile.
10. **Trigger guard:** a squared front.
11. **Left-side lever:** it is not on the real pattern, so keeping it (as an ambidextrous selector) moves the design
    away from the original rather than toward it.

## Open questions for the user

- **Magazine thickness.** The front close-up implies about 18 mm. A real 7.62x39 double-stack magazine is about
  28-30 mm, and the receiver is 43.5 mm wide in TOP. Build to the close-up, or to a plausible ~28 mm?
- **The left-side lever.** Build it as the LEFT view shows, or follow the real pattern (right side only)?
- **Barrel diameter.** The views read 14.8, 15.5 and 16.7 mm. Is 15.5 mm acceptable?
