# Assault Rifle AR-01: game and tech build plan

**Date:** 2026-10-04. **Role:** game + tech planning only. Nothing was built, no lock was taken, and neither Unreal nor
Blender was run.

and possibly Fab, as GUN_BUILDING_STUDY 1.1 sets it:

## 1. Lessons carried over (katana, senbon, Snow Flower, kunai)

| Keep (worked) | Avoid (failed or cost time) |
|---|---|
| A parametric generator per part. LOD1 and LOD2 come from the **same generator at lower resolution** with the same UV islands: no Decimate, no UV transfer, no pattern pop | Relief baked onto flat solids read flat at grazing angles. | `Grip` socket = pivot = primary hand; sockets only through the sidecar | Katana: hulls built from vertices left 6-30 mm holes between chord hulls. Here: hulls from **dense surface samples** (≤ 1 mm at LOD0), neighbours overlap 3 mm, and a build gate on a jittered 0.5 mm sampling |

| The seat-gap check is a gate from day one | Katana: the 1440p LOD-pop measurement was planned and **never run**, so the screen sizes stayed a proposal. Here it is a required gate (2.4) |
| Unreal harness: fresh processes, sha256 of the bytes, hull round trip, composed-socket check (≤ 0.001 cm) | Katana blade too dark: the first BC sat below polished steel's F0. Here: check BC against measured reference albedo and physical ranges before the look review |

## 2. Frames, pivots, dimensions, LOD switching

### 2.1 Rifle frame (Blender metres, modelled in mm; Unreal cm after export)

- **Origin = `Grip`.** This is the centre of the right hand on the pistol grip: on the grip's centre line in the
  mid-plane y = 0, 46 mm (set by H1 early on `MH_PlayerDefault`, R31) down the grip axis from where the
  grip meets the receiver. The spec computes it from the trace: the grip-axis midline (22.35 deg from vertical, fitted
  over Z -64..-116) meets the receiver at M (280.67, -36.0), so `Grip` = M (263.18, 0, -78.54). This follows the house rule (pivot at the grip
  point), and it is how UE weapons are usually attached: the weapon's origin snaps to a weapon socket on `hand_r`.
- **+X = toward the muzzle.** The bore axis is **exactly parallel to X**, at y = 0 and z = `bore_z`, so the `Muzzle`
  socket needs zero rotation.
- **+Z** = up (top of the receiver).
- **-Y = the rifle's right side** (selector, ejection port, charging handle).
- **Why these axes.** The pipeline maps Blender (x, y, z) to Unreal (x, -y, z) × 100 (`ue_socket_transform`; the
  katana's omote at Blender -Y read +Y in Unreal). So the rifle arrives in Unreal with **+X forward, +Y right, +Z up**,
  the engine's own convention for a weapon held by a character.
- **Blender front view (from -Y) shows the right side, muzzle to screen right.** This matches the sheet's top-left
  panel and the 3/4 front-right view directly. The back view shows the left side.
- **Sheet caveat (for the study and the user).**
  - The top-left panel is labelled "LEFT", but it shows the selector side with the muzzle to the right: that is the
    **right** side. It agrees with the "RECEIVER AND SELECTOR (RIGHT)" close-up and the 3/4 front-right view.
  - The top-right panel (muzzle left) **also** draws a selector lever. It is very likely a mirror of the top-left.
  - This plan treats the top-left panel as the right side. It uses the top-right panel only as the left silhouette,
    without the mirrored selector, unless the user says otherwise (open question 2).
  - **Confirmed by the study (spec R1):** handed features, the 3/4 view, c1 and the TOP knob all agree. The
    top-right panel is the true left side; its selector (and a short pressed groove) are the only differences.

### 2.3 Magazine frame and the reload path

- **Mag origin = the front-lug hook point** (spec M (429.0, 0, -16.0) = rifle frame (+165.82, 0, +62.54), *provisional*
  ± 5 mm). When seated, that point equals the rifle's `Mag` socket [was `MagWell`], with identical axes. So the seated
  mag is attached at `Mag` with **zero relative transform** (SnapToTarget).
- **How the AK-pattern mag goes in:** the front lug hooks first, then the mag rocks rearward until the rear lug
  latches on the catch. Two extra rifle sockets turn that into code with no angle-sign reasoning:
  - `MagRockStart` sits at the **same location** as `Mag`, rotated so the mag's base swings **forward** by the rock
    angle θ. Spec θ = 18° (*provisional*); the build sets it as the smallest angle at which the mag's rear top corner clears the
    catch by 2 mm with the lug hooked. In Unreal this is **pitch +θ** (nose-up sense: it takes the mag's -Z toward
    +X). In Blender it is `rotation_euler.y = -θ`. The sidecar writes the real value through `ue_socket_transform`,
    and the pipeline test confirms the sign.
  - `MagInsertStart` is `MagRockStart` moved d = 8 mm along the tilted mag's local -Z, before the lug enters its
    recess: rifle frame (+168.29, 0, +54.93).
- **Insert** = lerp the location `MagInsertStart` → `MagRockStart` (rotation constant), then slerp the rotation
  `MagRockStart` → `Mag` (location constant: the pivot is the hook). Removal runs the same path in reverse. Because
  the pivot is the mag's origin, the rock is a **pure rotation**, the same trick as the katana's `DrawPivot`.
- **Hand.** For the reload the mag is detached from the rifle and attached to `hand_l`. The skeleton socket's relative
  transform = inverse(`HandReload`), computed once in the game project.

### 2.4 LOD switching (measured, not defaulted)

- **Unreal's screen size** = 2 · max(0.5·P[0][0], 0.5·P[1][1]) · r / d. At 16:9 with a 90° horizontal FOV this is
  ≈ **1.78 · r / d** (P[1][1] = aspect / tan(hFOV/2)). With the spec's r = 0.450 m:
  - Screen size 1.0 at 0.80 m, 0.30 at 2.67 m, 0.12 at 6.7 m, 0.04 at 20 m.
  - Your own rifle in third person (camera ~3 m): ≈ 0.27, about 375 px long at 1440p.
  - The duel opponent at 5-10 m: 0.16-0.08.
- **First person always shows LOD0.** The first-person component uses a forced LOD 0 (`ForcedLodModel = 1`), so the
  first-person view never depends on screen size.
- **Start: 1.0 / 0.30 / 0.12 / 0.04** (LOD1 from ~2.7 m, LOD2 from ~6.7 m, LOD3 from ~20 m; gun study 5.7).
  - With the house 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.25, your own third-person rifle would sit on LOD2-ish detail at 3.2 m.
- **LOD-pop gate (required):** render each switch at its distance, 2560 × 1440, 90° hFOV, from 4 headings. Thresholds:
  - mean silhouette difference ≤ 1 px and max ≤ 3 px;
  - the rear sight, front sight ears and brake cut still read at LOD1 → LOD2.
  - If a threshold fails, the size moves (never the budget first). The chosen sizes go into the sidecar (an FBX
    LodGroup carries none; Unreal would otherwise compute 2.0 / 0.75 / 0.56).
- **The magazine LODs on its own bounds** (spec r_mag = 0.108 m). With the rifle's sizes it would drop to LOD2 at 1.3 m
  while the rifle is still on LOD0. So its sizes are **derived**: size_mag = size_rifle × r_mag / r_rifle, using both
  meshes' measured Unreal bounds. That gives **1.0 / 0.072 / 0.029 / 0.010** (re-derived from the measured Unreal bounds), so both switch at the same distance. The same
  sizes are right for a dropped mag on the ground. Gate: both measured switch distances within 5 %.
- **LOD3 is now in the contract** (gun study 5.7 and 11.7): 1.5-2.5k rifle, 200-300 mag, from about 20 m, for the
  battle royale. This answers open question 3.

## 3. Triangle budgets

### 3.1 Why these numbers
- **Gun study 5.7 sets the rifle at 30-45k LOD0 (ceiling 50k).** A first-person rifle is the most-viewed object in the
  game. LOD0 cost is bevel rings first (~10-15k), then rounds (32-48 sides), then fasteners.
- **Where the triangles go:** small cylinders (rivets, pins, screws), the sights and the brake, which are exactly what
  the first-person camera inspects. Plain stamped panels and the wood need few triangles once the bevels are crisp.
- **Cost:** in the duel at most 2 rifles are drawn, so LOD0 cost is negligible. In the battle royale the LOD chain
  (LOD3 now) carries the cost.

## 4. Construction rules that matter for the tech side

- **Shading.** Mid-poly with real bevels: 0.3-0.8 mm on machined edges, rolled radii of ~1.5-2.5 mm on the stamped
  receiver bends (from the sheet). Bevel with Harden Normals, then Weighted Normal with Keep Sharp. Custom split
  normals on the wood (as the saya lacquer) so curved furniture does not facet.
- **Tracing is the 2D outline authority** for the side and top views (the study's `trace/`). Every part's profile is
  generated from the traced curves at the spec scale. Section shapes come from the top view and the close-ups.
- **Every moving part is a separate closed solid** with its pivot, axis and limits from the spec (gun study 11.2):
  bolt carrier with charging handle, trigger, selector, mag catch, rear sight leaf, and the brake as `muzzle_device`.
  `SK_AR01` binds them rigidly to their bones; the `SM_AR01` twin joins them. No hammer is modelled (the sheet shows
  none); the dust cover is fixed (no bone, gun study 2.2).
- **Hidden surfaces get low texel density, not deletion**, wherever they show during the reload (the well interior,
  the bolt face through the port). They are deleted where they never show (inside the stock, under the handguards).
- **Closed solids** wherever a ray-parity test runs: the mag (fit gates) and the well walls. Every boundary loop is
  capped (katana finding 12).

## 5. Materials, slots, textures and the pack colour system

The finishes, slots and texture sets are the gun study's (5.8-5.10) as the spec records them (`materials`,
`textures`). This section keeps only what a builder needs on top.

### 5.1 Finishes (spec `materials.finishes`)

- **Black lacquer over steel** (metallic 0, sRGB #353535, roughness 0.40-0.55) on the receiver, dust cover, sights,
  barrel, gas parts, front sight block, brake, guard and bands; it wears in sharp chips to **bare steel** (metallic 1,
  #C3C3C1, roughness 0.25-0.40; sliding faces 0.15-0.25).
- **Bluing** (metallic 1, #787878) on the small parts: carrier, selector, trigger, catch, leaf, retainer lever, rod,
  swivel, pins.
- **Walnut-tone shellac** on the stock, grip and handguards (#6B3C27, grain and figure tones in the spec).
- **Magazine lacquer** #313131; **butt plate** lacquer densely scratched to bare steel.
- Calibrate against sheet crops under matched light: gate **L5** (colour).
- No markings, numerals, stamps, serials, selector letters or leaf graduations.

### 5.2 Slots

| Asset | Slot (index) | Master | Parts | Buyer control |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- Dark finishes are safe under the Metal-From-ORM brightness trap (gun study 5.9).
- Instances follow the pack's chain (master → `MI_AR01_<Slot>_Base` → `MI_AR01_<Slot>`), built under the
  `PackMaterials` lock with `run_build.sh` (stage 9). Additions only; no master changes.
- **Wood BC must reproduce through the recolour by construction** (one stain hue × the luminance detail); the pack's
  default capture must equal the shipped BC (p99.9 ≤ 2 levels). If the single-hue grain reads flat in the look review,
  the fallback is fixed-colour wood, and the user decides.

### 5.3 Texture sets and texel density (spec `textures`)

| Set | Size | Content | Density |
|---|---|---|---|
| `T_AR01_Metal_{BC,ORM,N}` | **2048²** (decided, R29) | all rifle steel | class A 90 px/cm (leaf, notch plate, slider, ears), B 60 (rear receiver, dust cover, rear sight block, selector, carrier), C 35, D 26, E 8-15; fill 70-78 % |
| `T_AR01_Furniture_{BC,ORM,N}` + `Recolour/T_AR01_Furniture_Detail16` | 2048² each | stock, grip, handguards | grip top and comb class B, the rest C; fill ≥ 65 % |

- **Why 2048 for Metal:** at class mid-points (A 90, B 70) the steel needs ~3.0 M texels, just over the ~2.9 M that 2048
  holds at 70 % fill; at B 60 (the hip-view need) it is ~2.4 M and the packer raises B toward 65 until the fill
  reaches 70-78 %. ADS on the dust cover (0.30-0.35 m) wants 70-82, so the cover reads ~15-25 % softer there.
- **Fallback (recorded, not default):** 4096 at A 100 / B 80 if the L7 first-person crops show the receiver or cover
  soft. It costs ~36 MB more for BC+ORM+N and fills only ~25 %; set a third-person LOD bias of 1. It is also the Fab 4K
  option (gun study Q13).
- **UV0:** the SK keeps every slot in 0..1; the SM twin moves Metal to u 0..1, Furniture u 1..2, merged mag u -1..0. No
  mirrored or stacked islands.
- **UV1:** an authored fallback pack; the gate is on Unreal's generated channel (gun study 5.8).
- **Maps:** BC sRGB; ORM linear (R baked AO, G roughness, B metallic); N DirectX (green flipped on write); power of
  two, full mips; `TEXTUREGROUP_Weapon` / `WeaponNormalMap`. qa's aggregate texel figure is approximate on mixed
  sets: the builder's per-class report is the authority, and qa's `--texel` target is the measured aggregate ± 25 %.

## 7. Collision

### 7.1 Hulls (`UCX_<LOD0 node>_NN`, ≤ 32 vertices each)

Built by the builder's own code from **dense surface samples**: ≤ 1 mm at LOD0, ≤ 2 mm at LOD1/2, convex hull dilated
0.2 mm, reduced by keeping a support polygon. Neighbouring hulls overlap by 3 mm. `make_ucx_hull`'s whole-object
collapse is not used.

| Rifle hull | Covers | Why split there |
|---|---|---|
| 00 | receiver, dust cover, rear sight block, selector, charging handle | a box-like solid |
| 01 | fore-end: both handguards with the barrel and gas tube between the receiver and the gas block | a hull of the parallel barrel and tube is a slab, so no phantom |
| 02 | gas block, exposed barrel, rod to the front sight block | keeps the gas block height out of the brake hull |
| 03 | front sight block + post + brake | a separate hull stops a wedge from the post tip down to the receiver |
| 04 | pistol grip + trigger guard | the grip is raked; merged with the receiver it would fill the trigger-guard bay |
| 05 | stock + butt plate | |
| 06 | the merged magazine (static twin only, while it carries the mag) | the mag's curve needs its own hull |

| Mag hull | Covers |
|---|---|
| 00 | top half of the curve (lugs, lips) |
| 01 | bottom half (floor plate) |

One hull for the whole mag would fill the curve's ~30 mm sagitta.

**Gates:**
- C1: 0 of an independent jittered 0.5 mm LOD0 sampling, and 0 vertices of any LOD, outside the hull union (build).
- C2: 0 of 300,000 LOD0 samples outside **Unreal's stored hulls** (round trip, katana method).
- C3: hull counts 7 (6 if the twin ships without the merged mag) and 2.

### 7.2 In play
- **In hand:** set the rifle and the mag to NoCollision or QueryOnly. The character owns its capsule; physics on a held
  weapon fights the animation.
- **Dropped:** simple collision with physics on.
  - Mass overrides: 3.1 kg rifle, 0.33 or 0.82 kg mag. CoM offset from `CenterOfMass`.
  - Collision complexity stays at its default (simple collision used for queries). There is no complex-as-simple: it
    would make the 30-45k-triangle render mesh the physics mesh.
- **A seated mag lies inside the well,** where the rifle's hull 00 covers it. When the rifle is dropped with a mag in,
  the mag's collision is off (it stays attached).
- Both READMEs state this.
