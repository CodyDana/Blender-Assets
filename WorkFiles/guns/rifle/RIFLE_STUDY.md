# Assault Rifle AR-01: reference and design study

**Date:** 2026-10-04. **Role:** study merge (measurement + craft + game/tech). Nothing was built; Blender and Unreal
were not run.

**Outputs:**
- `rifle_spec.json` is THE build spec: 33 parts, 18 rivets, 8 bones, 17 + 5 sockets, finishes, budgets, gates and the
  file layout (resolutions R1-R29, including the adversarial check in section 11). It
  is generated by `tools/make_spec.py`, which computes every derived number (the Grip origin, sockets, dust-cover
  crown, magazine floor plate and bounds) from the traced outlines.
- `RIFLE_DESIGN_SHEET.png` is the dimensioned design sheet (4400 x 4900 px, 4 px/mm), generated by
  `tools/design_sheet.py`. It has five panels: spec side view, traced reference, spec top view, traced top view and a
  magazine detail with the reload rock start.
- Inputs: `MEASUREMENT_NOTES.md`, `rifle_measured.json` and `trace/` (measurement); `CRAFT_STUDY.md` and
  `craft_parts.json` (craft); `RIFLE_BUILD_PLAN.md` and `rifle_interface.json` (tech). `GUN_BUILDING_STUDY.md` at the
  repo root is the line standard, and its section 11 lists what the rifle plan must change. `RIFLE_BUILD_PLAN.md` is
  updated to the spec's numbers.

**The reference** is `References/Guns/Rifle/rifle_sheetAB.webp` (1536 x 1024, AI-generated by the user). It is a
near-replica of the real stamped-receiver 7.62x39 pattern. The game build matches the sheet; a Fab version needs the
original changes in section 8. The sheet is the reference, and the build is judged against the traced outlines by
looking, by measuring and by a blind test.

## 1. Decisions (merge log; full text in `rifle_spec.json` "resolutions" R1-R29)

Rules: measurement wins on dimensions, craft wins on construction, and tech wins on frames, sockets and budgets. The
repo-root line standard overrides the tech plan where it decides.

1. **The LEFT-labelled panel is the gun's TRUE RIGHT side (R1).**
   - A side view with the muzzle on the right is geometrically a right-side view.
   - It shows the selector, ejection port, carrier, charging handle and retainer arm exactly where the real pattern has
     them. It agrees with close-up c1, the 3/4 front-right view and the TOP view's right-side knob.
   - The RIGHT-labelled panel is the true left side. It has one generator fault: a copied selector lever, plus a short
     pressed groove.
   - The measurement and craft notes read this the other way round ("controls mirrored onto the left"). The tech plan
     and the gun study had it right.
   - With R1, three apparent inconsistencies go away: the 3/4 view, c1 and c4 are all correctly labelled, and the
     checker panel and the rear-sight retainer agree.
2. **Two measurement values were wrong, and both are fixed (R2, R3).** In each case a sliver ring a few pixels wide had
   been labelled with the wrong part.
   - The dust cover top is **+32.7**, not 46.9: the traced silhouette crown has a median of 32.7 over X 275-445, and
     craft read +33.
   - The upper handguard is **106.9** long (X 534.0-640.9), not 153.8.
   - The adversarial check (section 11) found more of the same and the trace itself is now cleaned (R19): slivers are
     dropped or relabelled in `tools/trace_reference.py`, so the trace keys no longer carry them.
3. **Scale is 880.0 overall and 415.0 for the barrel (section 4).**
4. **The magazine is 26.0 thick (R32, superseding R5's 28.0).** Not measurable on the sheet: the front close-up c5 reads about 18-20, and the
   close-ups are not drawn to one scale (c7 draws wood beside the butt plate where TOP shows the plate flush, so its
   ratio to TOP does not carry over to c5). The real-pattern double-stack width is 28-30; 28 is chosen. The blockout
   stage confirms it on the camera-solved 3/4 view (spine width against the 41.5 receiver) before it is frozen.
5. **Barrel Ø 15.0 (R6).** LEFT, the authority for heights, reads 14.8-15.0. TOP's 16.7 is excluded because TOP inflates
   thin round parts. **Cleaning rod Ø 4.0 (R7):** the sheet's 3.4 ± 0.7 at the top of its range, and nothing bigger is
   invented.
6. **All stations come from the LEFT trace (R8).** Craft readings (± 4 mm) are kept only for construction.
7. **The asset set and names follow GUN_BUILDING_STUDY (R12, R13, R18).**
   - Assets: SK + SM twin + mag.
   - Sockets use its 5.3 names: `SupportHand`, `ShellEject`, `Mag`, `Sight`, plus `MuzzleSeat` and `Holster`. The
     plan's names are kept as `plan_name`.
   - Layout: `Exports/Guns/AR01/`, `Scripts/guns/rifle/`, `Assets/Guns/AR01.blend`.
8. **Finishes follow the gun study (R14).** Craft's metallic-1 steel at sRGB 60 is not physical. The receiver reads as
   lacquer, so it is lacquer. Craft's sampled hues and relative values stay as calibration targets.
9. **Budgets follow the gun study (R15):** 30-45k LOD0 (ceiling 50k), LOD3 now, and the Metal set sized by texel class.
   The plan's ~27.5k per-part table is the floor allocation. **Metal texture: 2048, class B at 60 px/cm (R29),** with a
   recorded 4096 fallback.
10. **Check fixes that moved geometry (R19-R27, section 11):** the rear sight leaf overhangs the dust cover (notch X
    449.9, sight radius 375.4); the front sight block starts at X 806.1; the trigger guard bottom is -68.4 and it starts
    at X 298.7; the handguard has two measured side slots per side; the trigger guard's top flange is explicit; the ear
    hole is a raised boss; the butt-plate screws are cross-slotted; the grip axis is 22.35° (fit over Z -64..-116, so
    the Grip origin moved 0.4 mm); `ShellEject` is on `body`; `SupportHand` sits on the wood and is provisional.

## 4. Scale

- **880.0 mm overall**, from the butt heel to the brake's bottom lip. LEFT then reads 0.82248 px/mm.
- **Why:** the real fixed-stock pattern and the prompt both give 880.
- **Check:** at that scale the sight radius is 375.4 (notch at the leaf's rear end, R22) against a real ~378
  (-0.7 %). The receiver face to the muzzle is 363.5, which fits a 415 barrel with 51.5 inside the trunnion. So the
  chamber face is at X 433.8, inside the port span.
- **Uncertainty:** ± 1.5 % globally.
- **The ruler is unusable:** its labels run 0-90 and then "90" again, which implies 108.5 or 97.7 cm.
- **Length of pull is 317 mm,** shorter than the real ~345: the sheet draws a short stock. Kept as drawn.

## 8. Fab version: original changes (NOT applied; the user decides)

The game build matches the sheet. For a Fab release the design should become the user's own. Pick four or five of
these; none changes frames, sockets or slots:

1. Muzzle: an original device (a round two-port brake, or a cylinder with 3 short slots) instead of the slant cut.
2. Dust cover: two lengthwise stiffening ribs and a stepped rear (or a 1913-profile rail on top, gun study Q7).
3. Receiver: its own rivet layout and a rectangular recess or long pressed rib instead of the oval dimple.
4. Stock: a straight comb without the nose step; an optional tang strap or inset cheek panel.
5. Butt plate: a rubber pad with 3 horizontal ribs and no screws.
6. Handguards: one palm swell with three shallow flutes instead of the two side slots; a flat band with one screw.
7. Grip: a horizontal-groove or hex-stipple panel instead of diamond checkering; a subtle finger swell.
8. Gas block: a squared front instead of the 45° sweep; round vents in a 2-2 pattern.
9. Front sight: a closed round hood, or ears with round windows.
10. Rear sight: its own leaf and slider profile.
11. Magazine: its own rib layout (two wide ribs + a cross-rib) and a stepped floor-plate lip.
12. Selector: a shorter, wider lever with a hooked tab (or an ambidextrous left lever, which is not on the real
    pattern).
13. Trigger guard: a squared front.

## 9. Open questions (only the user can decide)

1. **Left receiver side.** Build the real pattern: a plain wall with the dimple, the rivets and the drawn groove (the
   default). Or copy the RIGHT-labelled panel literally, with a second selector lever on the left. That lever is the
   only design-level conflict between the two side views.
2. **`SM_AR01` and the magazine.** Your request named `SM_AR01` as the rifle with a separate magazine mesh. The gun
   study's default (Q3) also merges a copy of the magazine into the `SM_AR01` static twin (for world pickups and drops),
   while `SM_AR01_Mag` stays separate for the reload. Keep the merged copy (the default) or ship `SM_AR01` without a
   magazine?
3. **Grip checkering on the first-person LOD0:** normal map only (the default) or real pyramids (~3.6k triangles per
   side)?
4. **Fab version:** which of the 13 changes to apply, if any.
5. **Gun-study defaults this spec relies on:** rig pilot (Q2), black lacquer finish, `Exports/Guns/AR01/` (Q20), and
   the Metal texture at 2048 (R29) rather than the 4096 fallback. Answering any of these differently changes the build.

Closed in this merge, with their stated defaults (the user can still override):
- butt-plate screws: cross slots, as c7 draws them (closed by the check, R24; a single slot stays a Fab option);
- magazine thickness (26.0 since R32, the physical minimum; not measurable on the sheet);
- barrel diameter (15.0);
- overall length (880.0);
- static vs skeletal (the gun study's rig pilot);
- leaf graduations (none);
- LOD3 (now).

## 10. Risks

- **Provisional values** are set by later stages: the magazine hook (± 5 mm, fit stage), the rock angle (18°), the
  bolt travel (100), the selector angles, the hidden pivots and the Grip's 40 mm (gate H1).
