Overall design

Varied branch routing and blossom groups, integrated woody swellings, increased local contour variation and made bark engraving visible on the relief surface. Retained proximal blossom clusters and the second group near 61–65% of blade length.

Guard

Manually placed reference landmarks define lower curved shoulders and narrower upright cupped leaves. Shorter thorn lace exposes the large dark central pendant. A seated raised crown and compact branch lattice frame the upper flower.

Blade relief

Varied branch routing and blossom groups, integrated woody swellings, increased local contour variation and made bark engraving visible on the relief surface. Retained proximal blossom clusters and the second group near 61–65% of blade length.

Pommel

Rebuilt as one rounded housing with its axis along Blender Y, measuring 36.59 mm across and 23.46 mm deep including relief. Recessed flowers decorate the front and back. Rounded shoulders fill the side silhouette and seat into the grip.
