# Snow Flower — reference revision 2 review

**Status: geometry and exports updated; exact visual identity is not certified.**

[Open reference / previous / revised comparisons](REFERENCE_REVIEW.html)

## Changes made

- **Guard:** Replaced rounded open loops with angular stepped shoulders, paired overlapping leaves, recessed thorn-and-scroll fields and a nested pointed pendant.
- **Blade relief:** Recomposed the blossoms into proximal clusters and a second group near 61–65% of blade length. Added a wider irregular trunk, short connected forks, knuckles, interrupted grooves and a rougher branch material.
- **Flowers:** Rebuilt petals as shallow cupped surfaces with varied contours, thinner rims, fine inner engraving and metallic stamen centers.
- **Grip:** Replaced the padded spiral with thin alternating over/under strips whose crossings face front and back. Added connected floral ornament and finer collar scrollwork.
- **Pommel:** Removed the separate side-disc assembly. Built one compact raked housing with concentric borders, front and reverse flowers, and a connected ornamental wreath. Adjusted placement to clear the neck.
- **Tassel:** Added braided cord and tied knots, a layered charm, a fuller curved bundle, staggered strand tips and loose edge fibers. Shortened the endpoint to approximately guard height.
- **Presentation:** Added direct cap illumination, reduced broad material cloudiness, and corrected camera near clipping for close-up inspection.

## Remaining limits

This revision is closer to the supplied design, but identical reference fidelity has not been established. The guard scrollwork and fine engravings are reconstructed rather than exact tracings; the branch contours and blossom shapes remain more regular than the concept. The reference cap is inconsistent across its front, back and detail views; the single raked, two-sided housing is an inferred construction. Reference lighting, perspective and hidden geometry cannot be recovered exactly from this sheet.
