## 1. Heirloom Haul: Physics Valuables Kit for Co-op / Extraction Horror

**Cluster:** horror-gothic · **Type:** Prop pack + Blueprint function layer (physics valuables with value/fragility data) · **Rank:** 15.0 (demand 5, gap 4, fit 5, effort M) · **Price est.:** $29.99-$39.99 (competitor prices not seen)

- **Contents:** 40-60 original hard-surface 'valuables' (vases, mantel clocks, gramophone, candelabra, music box, typewriter, globe, framed portraits, porcelain, trophies, radios, jewellery boxes) in 3 size/weight classes; each with UCX collision, LOD0-2, grab-point sockets, and a pre-fractured Chaos Geometry Collection variant for the fragile ones; a DataTable (base value, mass, fragility, damage thresholds, rarity); a BP_ValuableComponent that lowers value on impact, fires break VFX/SFX at the threshold and exposes events for any grab/carry system; a sample extraction zone that totals the haul; a recolourable master material (porcelain, brass, lacquer, silver) so each mesh ships in several variants.
- **Demand evidence:** Search results describe R.E.P.O. as the breakout co-op horror hit of 2025-2026, built on physics-based fragile valuables that lose value when damaged. Lethal Company is built on scrap with values. Blogs call extraction horror 'the fastest growing indie horror genre on Steam' (FEEDERS, UE5, 2026). The Thunderstore mod 'LethalCompanyValuables' shows players want more valuable variety. Download/sales numbers for any of these: not seen.
- **Gap (Thin):** I searched 3 times (co-op scrap/valuables pack, R.E.P.O.-like fragile valuables template, extraction horror loot value). Each piece exists separately on Fab (grab systems, Chaos destruction, loot tables, co-op templates), but I found no themed kit that combines physics-ready props with a value/fragility layer. I rated it Thin rather than Open because generic antique/decor prop packs almost certainly exist; that query was not run because the search budget ran out.
- **Differentiator:** It is the only kit sold as 'valuables': every prop already has mass, grab sockets, fracture variants and value-on-damage data, so it drops straight into any grab system, including the ones listed above. Recolour variants multiply the SKU count cheaply. The FBX/GLB also sells to Unity and Godot co-op devs. Replication-safe value state would be a strong bullet point.
- **Fit (Strong):** This is almost entirely hard-surface props, which is our strength. The pipeline already handles UCX, sockets, LODs and recolourable masters. Chaos fracture and one component Blueprint are modest Unreal work. No organic or cloth work.
- **Skeptic verdict: WEAKENED** (gap now Thin, demand 4): 5 searches, plus 1 forum fetch that was blocked. Two things weaken the gap claim: (1) complete R.E.P.O.-like templates are now on sale (MPFriendslop, ACHAI), and they ship their own grab items, extraction and scoring, so the 'value/fragility layer' is not unique. (2) Antique/vintage/vase packs and destructible prop packs exist separately. What I did NOT find is a large, themed, physics-ready valuables library with Chaos pre-fracture variants and a value/fragility DataTable meant to plug into ANY of these templates. That is the realistic product. Demand is real: R.E.P.O. is a hit, 'friendslop' is now a named genre with many 2026 listicles, and templates are appearing. Their buyers are exactly the people who will need more content. What would have to be true to sell: position it as a content add-on that is compatible with the popular templates (ship adapter notes for them), keep the props clean hard-surface (avoid ornate carved candelabra and clock relief, a weak area for us), and price it as a prop pack, not a system.
