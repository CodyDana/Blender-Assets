# Dojo kit: per-piece reference prompts for ChatGPT

**Date:** 2026-09-27. **Purpose:** close-up reference sheets for the dojo's signature pieces, so each kit is modelled
from clear views with known proportions. Simple modules (wall runs, slabs, posts, beams, crates) need no sheet: the
spec and the two overview images cover them.

**How to use**
- Start one ChatGPT image chat. Attach both overview images (`Downloads\dojo1_reference1.png` and
  `dojo1_reference2.png`) once at the start, so every sheet keeps their look.
- Paste the **shared rules** block first, then one piece prompt at a time, in the order below. One image per prompt.
- If a result adds anything from the "do not include" list, or changes the dimensions badly, ask it to fix that and
  regenerate.
- Save each result to `Downloads` as `dojo_<piece>_ref.png` (the name is given with each prompt) and tell me. I copy
  them to `References\Dojo\` and log them as AI-generated (Fab asks about AI use; these are modelling references only,
  the meshes are built new).

**Order** (it follows the build order): 1 wall → 2 gatehouse → 3 hall front → 4 roof details → 5 storehouse +
residence → 6 corridor → 7 drum pavilion → 8 training shed → 9 lanterns, well, cistern → 10 training props →
11 modern props.

**Dimensions** come from `DOJO_ARENA_SPEC.md` (they are gameplay numbers: climb heights, roof pitches). Where an image
drifts from them, the spec wins; the image is for look, detail and construction.

---

## Shared rules (paste once, before the first piece)

```
I am building a realistic video-game environment: an original Japanese martial-arts dojo compound, shown in the
two attached images. I will ask you for reference sheets of individual pieces, one per message, so a 3D artist can
model them. Every sheet follows these rules:

- Format: a clean "model sheet" on a plain light-grey background, even soft daylight, no scenery. Show the piece in
  a front elevation, a side elevation and a top view (orthographic, straight on, no perspective), plus one small
  3/4 perspective view. Keep all views to the same scale, side by side, clearly separated.
- Scale: include a plain grey 1.8 m tall human silhouette standing next to the front and side views. Keep the
  proportions to the dimensions I give.
- Style: exactly the look of the attached images - realistic, physically based materials, well kept but lived in.
  Palette: dark weathered timber, cream plaster, earthen tan wall plaster, silver-grey clay roof tiles, grey granite,
  pale sand, a few deep red accents at most.
- Show construction clearly: joints, tile rows, trim, how parts meet. No clutter, no props unless I ask for them.
- Do not include: any text, letters, numbers or signage; any crest, emblem, logo or symbol (I add my own emblem
  later); anything from Naruto or any other anime, manga or game (no leaf, swirl, sand, cloud or mist symbols, no
  forehead protectors); no real brand names or logos; no real family crests; no religious symbols; no faces or
  creatures on roof ornaments; no copy of a real named temple, castle or dojo; no people other than the grey scale
  silhouette.
Reply "ready" and wait for the first piece.
```
