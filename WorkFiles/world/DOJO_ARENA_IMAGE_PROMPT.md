# Dojo Arena: prompts for ChatGPT image generation

**How to use:** start a new ChatGPT image chat, attach `DOJO_ARENA_TOPDOWN.png` (same folder), then paste prompt 1.
The plan image carries the exact layout; the prompt tells the model to follow it and what the place should look
like. Use prompt 2 afterwards, in the same chat, for an eye-level view. The asset list is made later from the
result. If a result adds anything from the "do not include" list, ask it to remove that and regenerate.

---

## Prompt 1: top-down / axonometric concept illustration (paste as is)

```
Use the attached top-down plan as an exact layout guide. Create a high-angle axonometric concept illustration
(about 45 degrees down, looking north from above the main gate, so the whole walled compound is visible) of an
original Japanese martial-arts dojo compound used as a duel arena in a realistic video game. Keep every building,
wall, opening, prop and open space in the same place, size and proportion as on the plan. North is up on the plan.

Layout to follow (metres):
- A rectangular compound 44 m east-west by 36 m north-south, enclosed by a 2 m tall, 1 m thick earthen plaster wall
  with a stone footing and a grey clay-tile cap. The flat top of the wall is wide enough to walk along.
- Main gate in the middle of the south wall: a timber gatehouse with a tiled gable roof, 4 m wide, heavy wooden
  gate leaves.
- In the centre, a large empty fighting ground of pale raked sand, 28 m by 17 m, with a flush stone path running
  from the gate to the hall. Nothing stands on the sand.
- On the north side, the main dojo hall, 18 m by 10 m: dark weathered timber frame with cream plaster panels,
  sliding wooden doors and lattice windows, raised 50 cm on a wooden veranda that wraps the front and both sides,
  with a long stone step in front. Two-tier grey clay-tile roof at a moderate 25 degree pitch: a lower roof over
  the veranda and a larger hip-and-gable upper roof, ridge about 8 m high.
- North-west corner: a white-plaster storehouse with a tiled roof and a modern steel door. North-east corner: a
  matching residence / wash house. Each is joined to the hall by a short covered wooden corridor.
- South-west corner: a small training shed with a corrugated steel lean-to roof on steel pipe posts.
- South-east corner: a small open drum pavilion on a stone plinth with a pyramid tiled roof and a large taiko drum.
- In the gravel yards around the sand: wooden weapon racks, straw-wrapped training posts, wooden training dummies,
  two stone lanterns in front of the hall, a stone well in the east yard, one tree on each side (Japanese cypress
  or pine), low wooden crates, and a wooden water cistern at each front corner of the hall.
- Subtle modern touches, few and believable: two small air-conditioning units sitting on the lower roof at the
  front of the hall, a plain vending machine against the inside of the south wall, gutters and downpipes, electric
  lamps on brackets by the gate, a wooden power pole with overhead wires outside the south-east corner, and a
  narrow road with two street lamps outside the gate.
- Outside the walls: a few trees and a hint of a town edge; keep it quiet so the compound reads first.

Look: grounded, realistic, physically based materials, like a modern realistic game environment or a
photographic architectural model. Well-kept but lived-in: rain streaks under eaves, moss at the wall base, worn
steps, raked sand. Colours: dark weathered timber, cream plaster, silver-grey roof tiles, grey granite, pale sand,
muted greens, a few deep red cloth accents. Soft evening light with warm lanterns lit is fine; keep shadows
readable.

Do not include: any symbols, emblems, headbands or logos from Naruto or any other anime, manga or game (no leaf,
swirl, sand, cloud or mist village symbols, no carved faces on cliffs, no forehead protectors); no real brand names
or logos on the vending machine or signs; no real family crests; no readable text (use blank or abstract
signage); no copy of any real named temple, castle or dojo; no people or characters; no torii gates.
```

## Prompt 2: eye-level establishing view (use after prompt 1, in the same chat)

```
Now show the same compound from eye level, standing on the raked sand just inside the main gate and looking north
toward the dojo hall: the empty sand fighting ground in front, the stone path leading to the hall's long stone
step and wooden veranda, the two-tier grey tile roof with the two small air-conditioning units on the lower roof,
the stone lanterns, the water cisterns at the hall's front corners, the storehouse and residence roofs to the
left and right, and the 2 m tiled wall running around the edges. Same realistic style, same materials and the same
rules: no anime or franchise symbols, no brand logos, no readable text, no people.
```
