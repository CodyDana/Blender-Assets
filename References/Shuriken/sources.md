# Shuriken Asset Study, sources

**Date:** 2026-09-17. Companion to `SHURIKEN_STUDY.md`. Numbers match the `[n]` markers in that document.

Pages marked **read 2026-09-17** were fetched and quoted directly during this pass. Royal Armouries and Touken World pages are JavaScript shells and were read with a browser rather than a plain fetch. Nothing was downloaded: image entries record the page, the licence and the direct file URL only.

Source quality, in descending order of weight: museum catalogue records, living-school publications, Japanese encyclopedias, martial-arts school and glossary sites, then retailer listings. Retailers are useful for modern dimensions and useless for history. Where a claim rests on a single author or a single vendor, that is stated.

Eight entries below carry no inline `[n]` marker in the study: 9, 15, 27, 28, 33 and 35 supplied the min / typical / max range columns in section 2 rather than any single sentence, and 65 and 66 are project files that the study cites by filename and section instead of by number.

**Range-column numbers with no traceable source** are flagged SNIPPET in the study's own tables rather than left to read as measured figures. There are five: 34 g (§2.1 mass minimum) and 5.0 mm (§2.4 thickness maximum) come from retail pages recorded only as search summaries, and 44 g, 100 g and 180 mm (§2.8) come from a blocked retailer page and from the dead Delta2Alpha pages at [64]. Two more that were previously unattributed now have entries of their own: 53 g (§2.2 mass minimum) is Royal Armouries XXVIM.26 [67], and 45 g (§2.3 mass minimum) is a small Togakure senban listing [68].
