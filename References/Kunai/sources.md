# Kunai Asset Study, sources

**Date:** 2026-09-19. Companion to `KUNAI_STUDY.md`. Numbers match the `[n]` markers there.

**Read 2026-09-19** means the page was fetched in the verification pass and the figure used was quoted from it. **Research pass** means the figure comes from the research agents' reading the same day and was not re-fetched. **SNIPPET** means seen only in a search summary. Nothing was downloaded: no images, no files, no 3D models. Fab and ArtStation pages were read in a browser by the research pass, without logging in or accepting anything.

Weight, in descending order: primary text and its renderings, Japanese institutional and museum pages, encyclopedias and martial-arts schools, measured retail listings, blogs. Retail is good for modern dimensions and useless for history. Replica listings of the three-prong design carry franchise names in their titles; they are cited for measurements only.
